## Key concepts

### When to Use the Adaptive Approach
- Are the project requirements complex?
- Does the project involve risk?
- Is early feedback from customers needed?
- Is the organization receptive to the flexibility and risk tolerance the approach requires?

### Decision Criteria Categories
- Product/Service/Result-based: attributes of the expected outputs the project generates.
- Project-based: attributes of the project management.
- Organization-based: attributes of the organizational context of the project.

### Team Structure in Adaptive Projects
- Adaptive teams are "T-shaped": each member is outstanding in certain primary skills and also has broad capabilities in other skills.

### Requirements of the Adaptive Project Environment
- Adaptive mindset.
- Individuals and interactions over process and tools.
- Working software over comprehensive documentation.
- Customer collaboration over contract negotiation.
- Responding to change over following a plan.
- Structure and culture of adaptive teams: distributed management and leadership; a team may self-organize; a team member may act as facilitator (enabling communication, collaboration, engagement) instead of a designated project manager; limited team size and experienced team members.

## Key terms
- T-shaped team: members have outstanding primary skills plus broad capabilities in other skills.
- Product/Service/Result-based, Project-based, Organization-based criteria: as defined above.
- Facilitator: a team member who may serve in place of a designated project manager to enable communication, collaboration and engagement.
