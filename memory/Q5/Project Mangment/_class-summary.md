## 2026-10-15: Project Value, Quality, and Cost of Quality
Project value is total benefits minus total costs; predictive projects deliver most value after deployment, adaptive projects throughout the lifecycle. Quality is the degree to which inherent characteristics fulfill requirements, managed via plan, manage and control quality processes. Tools: reviews/inspections, fishbone diagrams, Pareto charts, control charts (3 sigma limits). Six Sigma means fewer than 3.4 defects per million opportunities. Cost of quality has four parts: prevention, appraisal, internal failure, external failure.

## 2026-10-19: Adaptive Approach in Project Management
Choose an adaptive approach when requirements are complex, there is risk, early customer feedback is needed, and the organization accepts the flexibility and risk. Decision criteria fall into product/service/result, project and organization categories. Adaptive teams are T-shaped (deep primary skills plus broad capabilities). The environment favors an adaptive mindset, the four "X over Y" preferences, distributed leadership, possible self-organization with a facilitator instead of a project manager, and small teams of experienced members.

## 2026-10-22: Adaptive Project Stages Concept to Delivery
Adaptive projects have three apparent stages: Concept, Construct and Deliver, Close. Concept produces a vision statement and a product roadmap (requirements with tentative release dates, stakeholder management plan, team charter). Construct and Deliver decomposes requirements into epics, features and user stories, does release planning, keeps a prioritized product backlog, estimates in hours or story points, and runs daily standups, an iteration review and a retrospective. The Close stage is not detailed in the notes.
