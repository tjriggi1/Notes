## Summary
The session gave three lessons for life success, statistics on why customer experience matters in sales, and real-business examples of the four experience realms.

## Key concepts

### Three Lessons for Life Success
- Have a great attitude. Blame no one.
- Work really hard. Put in the time and hours.
- Practice continually, over and over.

### Why Experience Matters
- 88% of customers say the experience matters as much as the product or service.
- 32% would leave a brand they love after one bad experience.
- A 5% rise in customer retention can lead to 25-95% higher profits.

### Experience Realms in Action
- Entertainment: live music, trivia nights.
- Educational: nutrition workshops in a gym, coffee classes, DIY decor workshops.
- Esthetic: local art in a hotel, cozy lighting in a cafe, a minimalist tech showroom.
- Escapist: themed reading nooks in a bookstore, VR trips at a travel agency, pop-ups set in a fictional world.
