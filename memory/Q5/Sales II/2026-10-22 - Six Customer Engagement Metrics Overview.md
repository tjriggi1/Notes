## Summary
The session introduced six metrics for measuring customer engagement: NPS, CLV, CSAT, CES, CWF and CM. Each views the customer relationship from a different angle: loyalty, value, satisfaction, effort, delight and emotion.

## Key concepts

### Six Customer Engagement Metrics
- NPS (Net Promoter Score): how likely customers are to recommend you; signals loyalty and growth.
- CLV (Customer Lifetime Value): total revenue expected from one customer; shows which customer segments to prioritize.
- CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score): immediate feedback on a specific interaction or product; reflects customers' overall happiness.
- CES (Customer Effort Score): how easy it is for customers to do business with you.
- CWF (Customer Wow Factor): surprise and delight that exceeded expectations and created memorable moments.
- CM (Customer Mood): emotional state of customers, individually and as a group.

## Background (not from notes)
- These points appear in the note only as added context, not from class.
- NPS is usually one 0 to 10 survey question; 9 to 10 are promoters, 0 to 6 detractors; score = % promoters minus % detractors.
- CSAT is usually collected right after a purchase or support contact, often on a 1 to 5 scale, so it is a snapshot.
- CSAT, CES and CM describe how customers feel now; NPS and CWF point to loyalty and word of mouth; CLV looks ahead to long-term revenue. Using several metrics gives a fuller picture.
- Customer engagement is the ongoing interaction and emotional connection between customer and business.
