## 2026-10-20: Customer Experience and Sales Success (two notes, same session)
Three lessons for life success: great attitude (blame no one), work really hard, practice continually. Why experience matters: 88% say experience matters as much as the product; 32% would leave a loved brand after one bad experience; a 5% rise in retention can lead to 25-95% higher profits. Four experience realms with examples: entertainment, educational, esthetic, escapist. The realms are not defined in the notes.
