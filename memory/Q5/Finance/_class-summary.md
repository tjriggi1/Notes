## 2026-10-29: Scrum Timeboxing and Adaptive Agile Approaches
Timeboxing sets a fixed maximum duration for Scrum events to keep them focused; the sprint is timeboxed to one month or less. The Sprint Retrospective (team inspects itself and plans improvements) gets 45 minutes per sprint week; the Sprint Review inspects the increment and adapts the product backlog. Kanban is a flow-based approach for unpredictable workloads or when sprints are not preferred. Disciplined Agile (specialty-skill teams, full delivery life cycle) and the Scaled Agile Framework (alignment across many Agile teams at team, program, enterprise and portfolio levels) were mentioned briefly.
