# Customer Experience and Sales Success

*Sales II · 2026-10-20*

> This session covered three lessons for success in life, statistics showing why customer experience matters, and examples of the four experience realms in action: entertainment, educational, esthetic and escapist.

## Three Lessons for Life Success

- Have a great attitude and blame no one.
- Work really hard by putting in the time and hours.
- Practice continually, over and over.

## Why Experience Matters

- 88% of customers say the experience matters as much as the product or service.
- 32% of customers would leave a brand they love after one bad experience.
- A 5% rise in customer retention can lead to 25-95% higher profits.

## Experience Realms in Action

- Entertainment: live music and trivia nights.
- Educational: nutrition workshops in a gym, coffee classes and DIY decor workshops.
- Esthetic: local art in a hotel, cozy lighting in a cafe and a minimalist tech showroom.
- Escapist: themed reading nooks in a bookstore, VR trips at a travel agency and pop-ups set in a fictional world.

## Takeaways

- Attitude, hard work and practice are the three lessons for life success.
- Customer experience matters as much as the product or service to most customers.
- One bad experience can lose a customer, even one who loves the brand.
- A small rise in customer retention can lead to much higher profits.
- Experiences can be designed around four realms: entertainment, educational, esthetic and escapist.
