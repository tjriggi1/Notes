# Six Customer Engagement Metrics Overview

*Sales II · 2026-10-22*

> This session introduced six metrics that sales and customer teams use to measure customer engagement: NPS, CLV, CSAT, CES, CWF and CM. Each one captures a different side of the customer relationship, such as loyalty, value, satisfaction, effort, delight and emotion.

## Six Customer Engagement Metrics

Customer engagement can be measured in several ways. This slide covered six metrics, and each looks at the customer relationship from a different angle.

- NPS (Net Promoter Score)
    - NPS measures how likely customers are to recommend you.
    - It signals customer loyalty and growth.
- CLV (Customer Lifetime Value)
    - CLV is the total revenue you expect to earn from one customer.
    - It shows which customer segments to prioritize.
- CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score)
    - CSAT gives immediate feedback on a specific interaction or product.
    - It reflects customers' overall happiness.
- CES (Customer Effort Score)
    - CES measures how easy it is for customers to do business with you.
- CWF (Customer Wow Factor)
    - CWF captures surprise and delight that exceeded the customer's expectations and created memorable moments.
- CM (Customer Mood)
    - CM is the emotional state of customers, both one by one and as a group.

*Added context (not from class):*

> NPS is usually collected with one survey question on a 0 to 10 scale. Customers who score 9 or 10 are called promoters, and those who score 0 to 6 are called detractors. The score is the percentage of promoters minus the percentage of detractors.

> CSAT is usually collected right after a purchase or a support contact. It often uses a 1 to 5 scale, so it is a snapshot of one moment and not a long-term measure.

> The metrics work on different time scales. CSAT, CES and CM describe how customers feel now. NPS and CWF point to loyalty and word of mouth. CLV looks ahead at long-term revenue. Using several metrics together gives a fuller picture than any single one.

## Key terms

- **Customer engagement**: The ongoing interaction and emotional connection between a customer and a business. The six metrics in this session are different ways to measure it. *(added, not from class)*
- **NPS (Net Promoter Score)**: A metric showing how likely customers are to recommend you. It signals loyalty and growth.
- **CLV (Customer Lifetime Value)**: The total revenue expected from one customer. It shows which segments to prioritize.
- **CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score)**: Immediate feedback on an interaction or product. It reflects customers' overall happiness.
- **CES (Customer Effort Score)**: A measure of how easy it is for customers to do business with you.
- **CWF (Customer Wow Factor)**: Surprise and delight that exceeded expectations and created memorable moments for the customer.
- **CM (Customer Mood)**: The emotional state of customers, both individually and as a group.
- **Customer loyalty**: The tendency of customers to keep buying from a business and to recommend it to others. NPS is used to signal it. *(added, not from class)*
- **Customer segment**: A group of customers who share traits such as needs, behavior or value to the business. CLV helps decide which segments to prioritize. *(added, not from class)*

## Takeaways

- There are six customer engagement metrics: NPS, CLV, CSAT, CES, CWF and CM.
- NPS is about willingness to recommend, and it signals loyalty and growth.
- CLV is the total revenue expected from one customer, and it helps decide which segments to prioritize.
- CSAT is immediate feedback on an interaction or product, and it reflects overall happiness.
- CES is about how easy it is to do business with you.
- CWF is about exceeding expectations with surprise and delight, and CM is about customers' emotional state, individually and as a group.
